# Roofing article intent and consolidation decision review

**Date:** 2026-11-03  
**Scope:** Six roofing-related informational and commercial articles, plus the high-impression San Diego agency-comparison page. Reviewed repository frontmatter, introductions, FAQs and H2 structure. This is not a redirect authorization.

## Strategic constraint

LILOs Growth serves trades, hospitality, specialized commercial businesses and other local clients. Roofing is a valuable *industry-specific content cluster*, not the agency's sole identity. Protect the high-impression comparison asset while making the overall commercial site represent multiple verticals.

**Main finding:** Five generic how-to articles substantially target the same task and share the same step sequence. The buyer-selection guide is distinguishable. The San Diego best-agencies roundup is different again: comparison intent and geography.

## Content-integrity issues observed

- Some generic how-to introductions describe roofing as unusually easy to rank locally or present fixed ranking timeframes as if established. These statements need evidence or removal.
- Multiple FAQs claim 60–90 days, 3–4 months or similar reliable ranking/lead outcomes. Outcomes vary; no general guarantee is credible.
- Some articles imply a posting/citation/review cadence mechanically triggers rankings; verify claims with Google's policies and avoid review manipulation or fabricated service areas.
- The best-agencies page currently positions LILOs as “Roofing-Focused,” and contains dated timing claims; **do not rewrite it blindly** given its observed visibility. Audit named competitor comparisons, evidence, editorial fairness, and relationship disclosures before editing.
- Content should point to the **commercial** `/services/local-seo`, `/services/google-business-profile-optimization`, `/services/web-design`, and `/local-seo-san-diego` pages only where they materially support reader needs.

## Recommended consolidation outcome — subject to URL evidence

Maintain **one authoritative roofing local SEO implementation guide** with original, verified value: GBP eligibility and category checks; accurate roofing service pages; meaningful location coverage; technical and mobile conversion paths; authentic reviews and relevant links; non-branded GSC baselines; qualified estimate and phone tracking; and decision criteria for hiring help.

Do **not** choose the survivor by the shortest slug, date, or article length. Before deleting or redirecting any of the five how-to pages:
1. Pull per-page GSC impressions/clicks **and query associations**, index status, and external backlinks.
2. Review full body text, useful unique sections, actual sources, and internal links to each page.
3. Select a winner using intent fit and existing search/link signals; preserve its URL.
4. Merge unique, accurate information and correct unsupported claims in the survivor.
5. Create exact permanent redirects for the other variants, update internal links and sitemap output, and check canonical/redirect behavior in preview and production.
6. Record publication date and monitor indexation, non-branded impressions, clicks and meaningful inquiries. Do not infer causality from short-term position movements.

## What this batch does not do

No URL deleted; no redirect added; no rankings guaranteed; no changes to the high-impression roofing agency-comparison article. Do not conflate a repeated topic with proved query-level cannibalization: the available GSC Pages export does not provide a query-by-page join.

## Next implementation gate

Once stronger per-URL evidence is obtained, start with **one five-to-one roofing how-to consolidation**, not a mass merge across buyer-intent and comparison pages. Keep `roofing-local-seo-company-guide` and the San Diego comparative roundup distinct.
