## What an electrician should expect from local SEO

A credible plan starts with genuine electrical services, actual coverage, and accurate business information. Examples might include electrical troubleshooting, panel upgrades, EV charging installations, lighting, and commercial electrical work, where the company genuinely provides those services.

Compare agencies on:

- **Google Business Profile (GBP):** Correct real-world name, category, hours, eligible location/service areas, and legitimate review practices
- **Service pages:** Useful, distinct content for the electrical work a contractor actually performs
- **Local targeting:** Real communities served, without duplicate city pages or fictional offices
- **Website conversion:** Working phone links, estimate forms, mobile navigation, and contact delivery
- **Measurement:** Relevant Search Console queries, GBP interactions, completed calls, booked estimates, and limitations of attribution
- **Ownership and governance:** Administrative access to the domain, website, analytics, business listing, and advertising accounts

Google explains local ranking using [relevance, distance, and prominence](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091). No provider can promise a specific Map Pack placement.

## Questions that matter for electrical contractors

1. **Which electrical services will be targeted?** EV chargers, panel upgrades, repairs, inspections, and commercial projects have different customer journeys.
2. **How will GBP rules be followed?** The business name, locations, categories, hours, and service areas must represent the real operation.
3. **What will be built or changed?** Request a page-level and profile-level scope, not merely a ranking dashboard.
4. **Can you show permissioned project examples?** Ask for dates, deliverables, actual results where documented, and clear measurement limitations.
5. **How will calls and estimates be tracked?** Separate a phone-button click from a completed conversation and a qualified job.
6. **Who owns the accounts and site?** Confirm access to the domain, analytics, Google Business Profile, assets and CRM.
7. **What do the price and terms include?** Clarify setup, recurring work, ad spending, third-party tools, approvals, cancellation and offboarding.

Avoid service providers that sell fabricated Google locations, review manipulation, interchangeable city landing pages, or guaranteed first-page rankings.

## Understanding cost and performance

The cost of SEO depends on the deliverables. Comparing a standalone GBP maintenance fee against a plan covering custom development, content, reporting and advertising will not produce a meaningful benchmark.

Ask providers for itemized proposals with specific deliverables and realistic project schedules. A scheduled website launch is a deliverable; a promise of first-page rankings within a certain number of weeks is not.

Search Console impressions and clicks can inform search visibility, but do not automatically indicate qualified inquiries. GBP interactions, phone-button clicks and call records should be interpreted carefully, then connected to actual quotes and booked electrical work where reliable records exist.
