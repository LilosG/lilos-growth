Do they create neighborhood-specific landing pages? Can they rank for "water damage restoration in Carlsbad" and "mold remediation in Escondido" separately, not just "San Diego restoration"?

Your website must convert emergency calls. Is it mobile-optimized? Does it load in under 2 seconds? Can they build a fast, conversion-focused site? ## How to Choose: Questions to Ask

1. **"How many restoration companies do you currently serve in San Diego?"** Real experience matters. An agency that's ranked 15+ restoration companies knows the vertical.

2. **"Walk me through your GBP optimization process for an emergency services company."** Listen for specifics: photo updates, post frequency, review response strategy, seasonal adjustments.

3. **"What's your average time to first ranking improvement for home service businesses?"** Honest agencies will say 3–6 months for organic, 30–60 days for GBP visibility.

4. **"Can you show me case studies from restoration companies you've ranked?"** Ask for before/after GBP impressions, call volume, and organic rankings. Avoid vague success stories.

5. **"How do you handle negative reviews?"** Restoration companies get complaints. You need an agency that responds professionally and helps you address legitimate concerns.

6. **"What's included in your monthly fee, and what costs extra?"** Some agencies charge separately for content, paid ads, or website updates. Know the full cost upfront.
