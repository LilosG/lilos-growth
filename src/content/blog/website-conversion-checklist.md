> **TL;DR:** Fix the **five blockers** that stop visitors from calling: unclear headlines, weak CTAs, slow pages, missing proof, and bad forms.

---

## The 10-point checklist

1. **Headline clarity:** “We do _[service]_ in _[city]_ — _[outcome]_.”
2. **Primary CTA:** Above the fold, repeats every ~2 sections.
3. **Phone + sticky CTA:** Click-to-call on mobile.
4. **Proof:** 3 testimonials + stars near CTAs.
5. **Service area:** Name the neighborhoods you actually cover.
6. **Photos:** Real team/equipment; avoid stock.
7. **Speed:** Sub-2.5s LCP; compress images.
8. **Forms:** 4 fields max; clear success state.
9. **NAP:** Consistent across header/footer/GBP.
10. **Internal links:** Service hub ↔ most profitable pages.

## AEO-style FAQs you can copy

**Do you offer same-day service?** Most weekdays—book before 2pm.  
**How do quotes work?** **Written estimate** before work begins.  
**Do you serve [Neighborhood]?** Yes—**[Neighborhood 1]**, **[Neighborhood 2]**, **[Neighborhood 3]**.
