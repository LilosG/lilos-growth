## The Map Pack Ranking Factors That Matter in 2025

Local ranking is still **relevance + distance + prominence**, amplified by **reviews**, solid **on-page**, **citations/links**, and steady **GBP activity**.

### Relevance

Correct **primary + secondaries**, rich **Services**, **service/city pages** that mirror GBP, plus posts/Q&A.

### Distance

Clarify **service area** and build **city pages** to win nearby markets.

### Prominence

**Reviews** (velocity + responses), **citations** (clean NAP), **local links** (partners, sponsorships, coverage).

### Activity

**2 GBP posts/week**, frequent photos, prompt Q&A, monthly profile audit.

### Simple Roadmap

1. Fix GBP core → 2) Build service + city pages → 3) Earn reviews → 4) Citations → 5) Local links → 6) Keep posting.

**Do posts move rankings alone?** They help with relevance/engagement; pair with reviews + pages.  
**How fast to rank in a new city?** Expect a ramp with localized pages, reviews, and a few local links.
