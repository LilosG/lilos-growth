## A Local SEO Service Page Template That Wins

Service pages turn rankings into **booked jobs**. Use this structure for speed, proof, and conversion.

### Structure (Copy/Paste)

**H1:** “[Primary Service] in [City]”  
**Lead:** 2–3 benefit bullets + click-to-call + badges  
**What You Get:** concise deliverables  
**Problems We Solve:** symptom bullets  
**Our Process:** 3–5 steps with timing  
**Pricing & Guarantees:** ranges/warranties  
**Service Areas:** neighborhoods + map  
**Projects/Photos:** before/after grid  
**Reviews:** 3–6 quotes (with permission)  
**FAQs:** top 4  
**Sticky CTA:** Call / Book

### Conversion Boosters

Trust blocks • short forms • fast images • internal links to city pages + supporting posts.

### Launch & Scale

1. Ship the main service in your primary city
2. Add supporting services
3. Expand to 3–5 priority cities with unique intros, photos, projects, FAQs
