## Why photos move the Local Pack needle

Photos don’t directly “rank,” but they improve the signals that do:

- Relevance — proof of specific services, brands, and neighborhoods.
- Prominence & trust — real trucks, uniforms, licenses, before/after.
- Engagement — better CTR and more calls from the profile.
- Freshness — regular updates show you’re operating now.

## The Home-Service Shot List (rank-ready)

Use this on every job. Aim for 5–10 photos per job.

1. Curb & approach — house number or suite sign.
2. Truck at property — logo + phone in frame.
3. Tech in uniform — license if relevant.
4. Problem close-up — the broken/damaged part.
5. Fix in progress — hands + tools.
6. After photo — same angle as #4.
7. Outcome proof — gauges/thermostat/clear water, etc.
8. Brand/part identifiers — model/serial, boxes.

## 10-minute on-site → publish workflow

**At the job (tech)**: capture the list; skip faces without permission.  
**Office/ops**: light edits; upload to **GBP → Photos → By owner**; create an **Update (Post)** with <100 words + CTA; reuse 1–2 photos on a related service/job page.

## Where exactly in GBP

1. Photos → By owner
2. Updates (Posts) — one highlight photo + short summary; CTA = Call/Message/Book
3. Products/Services — attach a representative photo to the service

## On-page SEO placements (your site)

- Job/Project pages (service + city): before/after + 100–150 words
- Service pages: 1–2 trust photos near conversion blocks
- Location pages: neighborhood context + vehicle-at-property shot
