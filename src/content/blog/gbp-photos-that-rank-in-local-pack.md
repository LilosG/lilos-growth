Most GBP galleries are an afterthought. Yours shouldn’t be.
This playbook shows you how to **capture, prep, and publish** photos so they influence both **ranking** and **conversion**:

- **Capture**: jobsite, exterior, team-at-work, gear, results.
- **Rename**: service + city + brand (e.g., `rodent-exclusion-jupiter-fl-blue-door.jpg`).
- **EXIF**: time, GPS, keywords (where appropriate).
- **Upload**: primary, cover, categories, and fresh posts.
  Do this weekly and watch your **views → calls** climb.
