## The Best GBP Categories for Home Service Businesses (2025)

Choosing the right **Google Business Profile (GBP)** categories is a fast, compounding lever for **Map Pack** visibility. Below you’ll find **primary + secondary** pairings by trade, a **10-minute audit**, and how to reinforce categories with **services, posts, photos, Q&A**, and matching **service pages**.

> Done-for-you help? See **[GBP Optimization](/services/google-business-profile-optimization)** or pair with **[Local SEO](/services/local-seo)**.

### Quick Answer (TL;DR)

1. One **primary** tied to your most profitable service.
2. **4–8** tightly related secondaries.
3. 15–40 **Services** customers actually ask for.
4. **2 posts/week** with real job photos.
5. **Quarterly** category review.

### Recommended Categories by Trade

**Plumbers — Primary:** _Plumber_  
Secondaries: Drainage service · Sewer service · Water heater installation · Water heater repair · Septic system service · Leak detection.  
Proof: pages for **drain cleaning / sewer repair / water heaters**; weekly job + offer posts.

**HVAC — Primary:** _HVAC contractor_ (or _Air conditioning contractor_)  
Secondaries: Heating contractor · Air duct cleaning service · Furnace repair service · Heat pump installer.  
Proof: seasonal offer posts; installs/maintenance photos.

**Electricians — Primary:** _Electrician_  
Secondaries: Lighting contractor · EV charging station installer · Solar energy contractor (if applicable).  
Proof: pages for **panel upgrades / EV chargers / lighting**; permits & timeline Q&A.

**Roofers — Primary:** _Roofing contractor_  
Secondaries: Gutter cleaning service · Siding contractor · Solar roofing contractor.  
Proof: storm inspection posts; tear-off/underlayment photos.

**Pest Control — Primary:** _Pest control service_  
Secondaries: Termite control · Wildlife control service · Mosquito control · Rodent control.  
Proof: seasonal pest posts; exclusion photos; safety FAQ.

### 10-Minute Category Audit

- Primary = profit leader; remove fluff secondaries
- Services list complete, plain-language
- Fresh photos monthly; **2 posts/week**
- Seed 3–5 Q&As; steady review velocity

## FAQs

**How often should I change categories?** Quarterly or when your offer changes.  
**Can I create custom categories?** No—use Google’s list; put specifics in **Services**.
