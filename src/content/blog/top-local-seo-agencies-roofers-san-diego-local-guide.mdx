## What roofing businesses should expect from local SEO

The right work depends on real services and coverage. Roof repair, replacement, inspections, and commercial roofing can have different customer journeys. A good plan should address business accuracy, useful service pages, Google Business Profile eligibility, authentic project evidence, and phone and estimate functionality.

Google explains local visibility primarily through [relevance, distance, and prominence](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091). No agency can guarantee a particular ranking or timetable. Avoid invented business offices, keyword-stuffed business names, manufactured reviews, and duplicate city pages.

## Questions every roofer should ask before signing

1. **Which roofing services and cities are in scope?** Actual operating areas should determine service and local content.
2. **Which pages and GBP fields will you change?** Require an implementation plan, not only a keyword report.
3. **Can you show relevant work?** Request permissioned examples with dates and clear measurement context.
4. **How will you report leads?** Distinguish Search Console impressions, profile interactions, connected calls, qualified estimates, and booked jobs.
5. **Who owns the assets?** Confirm control of your domain, website, GBP, analytics, advertising accounts, and created content.
6. **What is included in the fee?** Separate setup, ongoing SEO, development, paid advertising, content, reporting, and any third-party charges.
7. **What are your cancellation and offboarding terms?** Know what remains available if the engagement ends.

An agency making guaranteed ranking or revenue claims should provide evidence and disclose limitations; Google results are not directly controlled by vendors.

## How to evaluate costs and results

There is no dependable universal price for “roofing SEO” without specifying the work. A GBP maintenance proposal is not equivalent to a full technical rebuild, ongoing content production, and paid media management.

Ask for a written scope, reporting sample, approval process, service ownership, and timeline for specific *deliverables*. Do not confuse deliverable dates with guaranteed lead or ranking outcomes.

Search impressions help diagnose visibility. Business Profile interactions and website phone clicks show actions but do not necessarily mean completed conversations. Qualified leads and booked work require reliable customer records and transparent attribution limitations.
