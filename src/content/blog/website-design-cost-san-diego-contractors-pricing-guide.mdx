If you're a contractor in San Diego shopping for a website, you've probably seen prices all over the map. Five hundred bucks. Five thousand. Fifty thousand. It's confusing, and most quotes don't explain what you're actually paying for.

Here's the truth: a proper website for a home service business isn't cheap, but it's not a luxury either. It's a lead-generation tool. You need to know what it costs, what you get, and whether it'll actually bring in work.

Let's break down real pricing for San Diego contractors and help you figure out what makes sense for your business.

## What You're Paying For: The Real Cost Breakdown

A website isn't just a digital brochure anymore. Customers expect it to be fast, mobile-friendly, easy to navigate, and trustworthy. That costs money to build right.

**Base Website Build: $3,500–$5,000**

This is your foundation. You get a professional 5–7 page site built on Astro with Tailwind CSS. That means:

- Lightning-fast load times (Google ranks fast sites higher)
- Mobile-first design (70% of your leads come from phones)
- Professional design that doesn't look like a template
- Basic contact forms and service pages
- SSL certificate and hosting included for the first year

You're not getting a Wix template or a WordPress site that breaks every time you update a plugin. You're getting a clean, modern build that works.

**Custom Features & Integrations: $1,500–$3,000 additional**

This is where your website becomes a lead machine. Common add-ons:

- **Lead capture forms** ($300–$500): Multi-step forms that qualify leads before they contact you. Plumbing emergency? Roof damage? Different forms for different services.
- **Service area pages** ($800–$1,500): Individual pages optimized for each neighborhood you serve. "Emergency plumber in Carlsbad," "Electrician in Encinitas," etc. This is local SEO gold.
- **Before/after galleries** ($400–$800): Contractors close jobs faster with visual proof. Restoration companies especially need this.
- **Online booking integration** ($600–$1,200): Customers can schedule estimates or callbacks without calling. Reduces friction.
- **GBP integration** ($500–$1,000): Your website syncs with your Google Business Profile. Reviews pull automatically. Consistency across search results.

## The SEO Question: Is It Worth the Money?

A beautiful website nobody finds is expensive decoration.

Local SEO for contractors typically runs $1,500–$3,000 for initial setup, then $500–$1,500/month for ongoing optimization. Here's what you get:

- Keyword research specific to San Diego (and surrounding areas like Oceanside, Encinitas, Carlsbad)
- On-page optimization for your service pages
- Technical SEO (site speed, structured data, mobile performance)
- Content strategy tied to what your customers actually search for
- Monthly reporting on rankings and traffic

For a plumbing company in North County San Diego, this means ranking for "emergency plumber Carlsbad" and "24-hour plumber Oceanside." That's real money in your pocket.

Without SEO, your website is invisible. With it, you're competing for leads that are actively searching.

## Real ROI Math for Contractors

Let's say you're a plumber in San Diego. Your average job is $1,200. You need 2–3 jobs per month from your website to break even on the investment.

- Website build: $5,000
- SEO setup: $2,000
- Total investment: $7,000
- Break-even: 6 jobs at $1,200 each

If your website generates 3 jobs per month, you've paid for itself in 2 months. After that, it's profit.

Most contractors we work with see 4–8 qualified leads per month from a properly built and optimized site. That's $4,800–$9,600 in monthly revenue from a one-time $7,000 investment.

Compare that to Google Ads, where you're paying $15–$50 per click with no guarantee of a conversion. Or door hangers, which cost money every time and don't scale.

## Add-Ons That Actually Matter

Not every contractor needs every feature. Here's what's worth the money:

**Worth it:**
- GBP integration (you're already on Google; might as well connect it)
- Service area pages (if you cover multiple neighborhoods)
- Lead forms (qualifies calls before they come in)
- Mobile optimization (non-negotiable)

**Maybe later:**
- Online booking (good if you do free estimates; less critical if you're appointment-heavy)
- Blog/content marketing (important for SEO, but not day-one)
- Video integration (nice to have; doesn't drive leads on its own)

**Skip it:**
- Fancy animations (slows your site down)
- Social media feeds embedded on your site (people go to social for that)
- Outdated "contact us" pages with no clear CTA (drives people away)

## Comparing Astro/Tailwind to Other Options

You'll see quotes for WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, and custom builds. Here's why Astro/Tailwind wins for contractors:

**WordPress ($2,000–$4,000):** Flexible, but slower. Requires ongoing maintenance and security updates. Plugins break. Not ideal for lead generation.

**Wix/Squarespace ($50–$300/month):** Easy to build yourself, but limited customization. Slow. You're renting, not owning. Over 3 years, you've paid $1,800–$10,800 with nothing to show for it.

**Custom builds ($10,000+):** Overkill for most contractors. You're paying for features you don't need.

**Astro/Tailwind ($3,500–$8,000):** Fast, modern, owned by you. One-time cost. Built for conversion. You own the code.

## What Affects Your Final Price

A few things will push your estimate up or down:

- **Number of service pages:** More services = more pages to optimize and design.
- **Custom integrations:** Connecting to your CRM or scheduling software adds time.
- **Rush timeline:** Want it done in 2 weeks instead of 4? Expect a rush fee.
- **Existing content:** If you have professional photos and copy ready, it's faster and cheaper. If we're starting from scratch, it takes longer.
- **Revisions:** Unlimited revisions during build are included. Major scope changes aren't.

## The Hidden Cost Nobody Talks About

Your website isn't a set-it-and-forget-it tool. You need:

- **Monthly hosting and maintenance:** $50–$150/month
- **SSL certificate renewal:** Usually included in hosting
- **Content updates:** You should be adding new reviews, photos, and service info regularly
- **GBP management:** 15 minutes per week to stay active
- **SEO maintenance:** If you're doing it right, $500–$1,500/month ongoing

Budget for this from day one. It's not a one-time cost.
