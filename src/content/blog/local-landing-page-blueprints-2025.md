> **TL;DR:** High-performing local landing pages pair one clear intent (Service + City) with tight sections: proof-rich hero, problem/solution, exact service steps, pricing ranges, local credibility, FAQs, and strong internal links. Use the blueprint, fill in with real jobs, and keep wording identical to your GBP services.

## Quick Answer

A local landing page should target a **single intent** like “water damage restoration in Encinitas” or “German roach treatment in Jupiter.” The winning pattern is: short, scannable **answer near the top**, then **evidence and detail** below. Mirror wording from your **GBP primary/secondary categories** and **Services** fields, and interlink to your **service hub**, **city hub**, and **related proof posts**.

---

## The 9-Section Blueprint (Service + City)

Use these sections in order and keep copy tight. Replace the bracketed tokens with your details.

1. **Hero with Offer + Proof**

- One-sentence promise and CTA.
- 3 credibility chips (years, guarantee, response time).
- Optional sub-CTA for estimates.

2. **Local Problem → Your Specific Solution**

- Name the common local cause: climate, building type, season.
- 3–5 bullet benefits tied to the city.

3. **Service Steps (What Happens After They Contact You)**

- A numbered 4–6 step list written in plain language.

4. **Pricing & Expectations**

- Give a **range** or **minimums** and list variables that change price or time.

5. **Before/After or Results**

- 2–3 quick examples with one-line captions.

6. **Local Credibility Signals**

- Neighborhoods served, affiliations, permits, insurance, or licensing.

7. **Guarantees & Safety**

- State what you guarantee and when re-service applies.

8. **FAQ (City-Intent)**

- 4–6 Q&As based on PAA and your call logs.

9. **CTA + Secondary Actions**

- Phone, text, and form CTAs; reinforce next available slots.

## 3×3 Local Landing Page Matrix

Use this to plan your build order and internal links.

| Intent             | Core page                         | Proof page                                          | Helper page                                            |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Service Hub**    | `/services/[service]/` overview   | Case studies: `/projects/[service]-[neighborhood]/` | FAQs: `/faq/[service]/`                                |
| **City Hub**       | `/service-areas/[city]/` overview | Testimonials tagged `[city]`                        | Neighborhoods: `/service-areas/[city]/[neighborhood]/` |
| **Service + City** | `/[service]-[city]/` (this page)  | Before/after gallery                                | Pricing explainer: `/pricing/[service]/`               |

**Linking rules**

- From **Service + City** pages, link **up** to the Service Hub and **across** to the City Hub.
- Link **down** to relevant case studies and back **up** again.
- Keep anchor text literal: “ant control in [City]”, “water damage restoration in [City]”.

## FAQs

**Do I need a separate page for every city?**  
Only for cities where you truly serve regularly and can provide proof and unique examples.

**Can I combine multiple services on one city page?**  
Yes for a city hub, but keep **Service + City** pages focused on a single service.

**What if pricing varies a lot?**  
Give a realistic range and list variables; add a link to your pricing explainer.
