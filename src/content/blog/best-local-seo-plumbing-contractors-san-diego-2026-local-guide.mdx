## Why North County Plumbers Need Local SEO

San Diego's plumbing market is fragmented. A contractor in Carlsbad competes against 40+ other plumbers within a 5-mile radius. A contractor in Oceanside faces similar saturation. Generic SEO won't cut it.

Here's what actually works:

1. **Hyper-local keyword targeting.** "Emergency plumber Carlsbad" and "24-hour plumber Encinitas" convert better than "plumber San Diego." You need an agency that understands this distinction.

2. **Service area optimization.** Your Google Business Profile needs to list every neighborhood you service, and your website needs location pages for each. Most national agencies skip this or do it poorly.

3. **Review velocity.** Plumbers with 50+ recent reviews rank higher than those with 10 old reviews. You need an agency that actively manages your review flow.

## Red Flags to Avoid

Before you sign a contract, watch for these:

1. **"Guaranteed rankings."** No legitimate agency guarantees Google rankings. If they promise top 3 in 30 days, they're lying or using black-hat tactics.

2. **Long-term lock-ins without results.** A 12-month contract makes sense if you're seeing results in 90 days. If you're not, you're trapped.

3. **No transparency on pricing.** If an agency won't tell you their base price upfront, they're hiding something.

4. **Generic content.** If they're writing the same blog posts for every plumber in every market, they're not doing SEO. They're checking boxes.

5. **No local market knowledge.** Ask them about North County neighborhoods. If they can't name three specific areas where your competition is strong, they don't know your market.
