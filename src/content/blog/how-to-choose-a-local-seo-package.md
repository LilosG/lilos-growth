Picking a Local SEO package shouldn’t feel like gambling.
This guide breaks down the **5 things that consistently drive local rankings and leads** for home-service companies:

1. **Foundations** — NAP consistency, site health, indexing, crawl.
2. **GBP** — Categories, products/services, photos, posts, Q&A.
3. **Pages** — High-intent service & service-area pages with strong on-page.
4. **Links** — Citations, local mentions, and contextual links that _fit your market_.
5. **Proof** — Reviews, before/after, case studies, and trust signals.
   By scoring vendors against these pillars, you’ll avoid “activity theater” and invest where it counts.
