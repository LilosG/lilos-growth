## What to Look for in Other Local SEO Agencies

If you're comparing agencies or want to understand what makes a good fit, here's what matters:

**1. Experience with home service businesses**

Generic SEO agencies often don't understand the plumbing business. They don't know that emergency calls are worth more than routine maintenance, or that service area radius matters more than citywide rankings. Look for agencies with a track record of ranking plumbers, electricians, or similar trades.

**2. Transparent reporting and access**

You should have access to your Google Business Profile, Google Search Console, and rank tracking. If an agency refuses to give you login credentials or hides reporting behind a dashboard you can't verify, walk away. You own your business—you should own your data.

**3. GBP management, not just SEO**

Google Business Profile is 60% of local SEO for plumbers. An agency should spend time optimizing your profile, posting regularly, managing photos, and responding to customer questions. If they're only doing website keyword optimization, you're missing the biggest opportunity.

**4. Review management**

Reviews drive clicks and conversions. Good agencies have a system for collecting reviews from past customers and responding to negative reviews professionally. This shouldn't be an add-on—it should be part of the core service.

**5. Local market knowledge**

An agency that knows Oceanside's neighborhoods, service areas, and competition will make smarter decisions than one that's generic across Southern California. Ask them about plumbing demand in Oceanside vs. Carlsbad, or which neighborhoods have the most calls. If they can't answer, that's a red flag.

## How to Choose Between Agencies

1. **Ask for case studies.** Request examples of plumbing or home service businesses they've ranked in Oceanside or nearby areas. Real results beat promises.
2. **Check their own rankings.** If a local SEO agency isn't ranking well for "local SEO Oceanside" or "SEO agency San Diego," why should you trust them to rank you?
3. **Talk to their clients.** Ask for 2–3 references from plumbers they've worked with. Call them. Ask about results, communication, and whether they'd hire the agency again.
4. **Understand the contract.** Month-to-month is better than 12-month commitments. You should be able to leave if results aren't happening.
5. **Clarify what's included.** Does the price cover GBP management, website optimization, review management, or just one of those? Ask for a detailed scope of work.
