## How to Choose the Right Local SEO Agency for Your Restoration Company

### 1. Ask for Restoration-Specific Case Studies

Don't accept generic "home service" case studies. A plumbing agency's playbook doesn't translate to restoration. Ask for proof: screenshots of GBP visibility improvements, lead volume increases, or review growth for restoration companies specifically.

Lilos Growth publishes case studies with Tamarack Restoration showing 40%+ visibility improvement in 90 days and measurable lead increases. That's the standard to expect.

### 2. Evaluate GBP Management Depth

Your Google Business Profile is your storefront. An agency should:

- Update your profile within 24 hours of any service area or availability changes
- Post 2–4 times per month with project photos and emergency messaging
- Respond to all reviews (positive and negative) within 24 hours
- Monitor and correct incorrect citations that harm local rankings

Ask how often they update your profile and whether they handle review responses. If they can't answer clearly, move on.

### 3. Understand Their Approach to Reviews

Restoration companies get emotional reviews—both glowing (homeowner is grateful their house was saved) and critical (customer is upset about cost or timeline). An agency should have a strategy for:

- Requesting reviews immediately after job completion (highest satisfaction window)
- Responding professionally to negative reviews without being defensive
- Using review data to identify service gaps

### 4. Check Local Market Knowledge

Ask about their experience in your specific North County cities. Do they know that Carlsbad has different search behavior than Oceanside? Can they speak to seasonal trends (higher water damage claims in winter, fire damage in fall)? An agency that knows your market will prioritize differently than one treating North County as generic San Diego.

### 5. Demand Transparent Pricing

Avoid agencies that won't quote upfront or bundle services into vague "packages." You should know exactly what you're paying for: GBP management, review generation, citation building, content creation, rank tracking—each with a clear cost. Month-to-month agreements are preferable; long-term contracts should include performance guarantees.

## Local SEO Best Practices Specific to North County Restoration

### Service Area Optimization

North County spans distinct markets: Carlsbad (affluent, older homes), Encinitas (coastal, weather-prone), Oceanside (larger, more diverse). Your GBP and website should reflect these differences. A good agency will optimize for "water damage restoration Carlsbad" differently than "emergency mold cleanup Oceanside."

### Seasonal Messaging

Winter brings water damage claims. Fall brings fire risk. Summer brings humidity and mold. Your local SEO strategy should shift messaging and content to match seasonal demand. Lilos Growth rotates GBP posts and content focus with North County seasonal patterns.

### Emergency Availability Signaling

Homeowners searching "24-hour restoration near me" need to know you're available now. Your GBP should clearly state hours, emergency response time, and service area. Your website should have a prominent call-to-action above the fold. An agency should audit this quarterly.

### Citation Consistency Across Emergency Directories

Restoration companies appear in specialized directories (ServiceMaster, IICRC, local contractor boards) in addition to Google, Yelp, and general business listings. Citation inconsistencies across these platforms hurt local rankings. A thorough agency audits all citations quarterly and corrects errors.

## Red Flags: What to Avoid

- **Agencies that promise #1 rankings:** Local search doesn't work that way. No one ranks #1 for everyone. Legitimate agencies promise top-5 visibility in your service area.
- **No performance metrics:** If an agency won't provide monthly reports with lead data, rankings, and GBP stats, they're not accountable.
- **Long-term contracts without performance guarantees:** Month-to-month or 90-day trial periods are standard in legitimate local SEO. Beware of 12-month contracts with no escape clause.
- **Generic "home service" approach:** Restoration is different. An agency treating it like plumbing is missing critical nuances.
- **Slow response times:** In restoration, urgency matters. An agency that responds in 48 hours is too slow.
